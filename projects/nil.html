02 / Case study

NIL

A short horror film shot in the woods at dusk. I filmed and framed every shot, helped direct and worked on the edit in post.

RoleCinematography, co-director, post-production
Team5 people
ToolsCamera, Premiere Pro
WhenGroup short film

Framing the fear

Horror lives in what you can't see, so most of my framing decisions were about hiding things: tight close-ups, figures left small in wide shots, and the cold blue of dusk that makes the forest feel empty even when it isn't.

05 / About

I grew up in the UAE around people from everywhere, and I think that's why I care so much about who a design is actually for.

I finished a BSc in Creative Computing at Bath Spa University in Ras Al Khaimah, then moved to Toronto on my own to study Interactive Media Design at Seneca Polytechnic. I don't want to be boxed in as just a UI/UX designer. UI/UX is one tool, next to branding, print, photography, film and sound.

Off the screen you'll find me behind a camera. I filmed and framed every shot of a short horror film, and I shoot portraits, cars at night and events whenever I can.
